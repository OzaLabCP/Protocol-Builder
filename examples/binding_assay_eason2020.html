In vitro fluorescence binding assay for gSTEP biosensor Kd determination

Source: Eason et al., ACS Synth. Biol. 2020 — 10.1021/acssynbio.0c00407

Measure the equilibrium dissociation constant (Kd) and fractional fluorescence change (ΔF/F0) between a gSTEP fluorescent-biosensor variant and its binding partner by titrating the partner against a fixed concentration of gSTEP in a 96-well plate and fitting the fluorescence response to a ligand-depletion Hill equation.

Estimated duration: ~3 h assay (excludes upstream protein expression/purification)

Procedure

  1. 1

    Prepare assay buffer

    stated

    Prepare 20 mM sodium phosphate buffer containing 50 mM NaCl and adjust to pH 7.4. Filter-sterilize. This is both the protein storage buffer and the assay diluent.

    Stated verbatim in the source.

    Prepare fixed-concentration gSTEP working solution

    stated

    Dilute purified gSTEP variant to 75 nM in assay buffer. Prepare enough for every well plus dead volume.

    gSTEP concentration75 nM stated
    Stated.
    gSTEP working-solution volume~8 mL default verify
    Scaling: 12 titrant points x 3 replicates x 2 (target + control) = 72 wells; 72 wells x 100 uL/well = 7.2 mL, +10% dead volume = ~8 mL. Verify against your plate layout.
    ⚠ Keep gSTEP on ice and protected from light until dispensed to limit photobleaching.
  3. 3

    Prepare the titrant serial dilution series

    user input

    Prepare a serial dilution series of the binding partner (STEPtag) spanning the expected Kd, plus a matched series of the control protein (BSA). Twelve points is a good default.

    3.1 Set the top concentration to ~10x the expected Kd; perform 2-fold serial dilutions down to ~0.1x Kd. best practice
    3.2 Prepare an identical dilution series of the control protein (BSA) to define nonspecific/background fluorescence change. stated

    User chose a Kd-relative range (recommended default). Requires an estimate of the expected Kd (from a pilot or the paper's reported values); flag to verify once Kd is known.
    Number of titration points12 points best practice
    12-point, 2-fold serial dilution is standard for a well-resolved binding isotherm.
    ⚠ If the expected Kd is unknown, run a wide pilot range first (e.g., 1 nM to 10 uM) and refine.
  4. 4

    Assemble the plate

    stated

    Into each well of a Fluotrac 96-well plate, combine gSTEP at 75 nM final with one titrant concentration, in assay buffer, to the chosen well volume. Include the control-protein titration and buffer-only blank wells. Run each condition in at least three replicates.

    Blank and gSTEP-only wells define F0 and background; standard practice for ΔF/F0.
    ⚠ Mix gently and avoid bubbles, which corrupt fluorescence reads.
  5. 5

    Equilibrate

    best practice

    Incubate the assembled plate to allow binding to reach equilibrium before reading.

    ⏱ 30 min · 🌡 25 C
    Equilibration time and temperature30 min at 25 C best practice
    Not stated in the source. User accepted the standard-practice default (equilibrium binding at controlled temperature). The ligand-depletion Hill fit assumes equilibrium, so this is outcome-critical — verify equilibrium is reached for your Kd/kinetics.
    ⚠ If on/off kinetics are slow relative to 30 min, extend the equilibration or the fitted Kd will be biased.
  6. 6

    Read fluorescence

    stated

    Read gSTEP fluorescence for every well on the Tecan Infinite M1000 with excitation at 485 nm and emission at 515 nm.

    Not stated. Set gain once on a bound (high-signal) well and hold it constant; verify no wells saturate.
  7. 7

    Fit Kd and ΔF/F0

    stated

    For each titration, subtract the control-protein background, then fit gSTEP fluorescence intensity as a function of titrant concentration to the ligand-depletion form of the Hill equation to extract Kd and ΔF/F0. Report mean +/- s.d. across replicates.

    Fitting modelHill equation accounting for ligand depletion stated
    The source specifies this fit (and cites its reference 35). Use total, not free, concentrations because ligand depletion is modeled.
    ⚠ Ligand depletion matters when Kd is comparable to protein concentration (75 nM here) — do not substitute a free-ligand Hill fit.

Open questions

  • Expected Kd is required to set the titrant range concretely; run a wide pilot (e.g., 1 nM to 10 uM) if unknown.
  • Equilibration time (30 min) is a best-practice default, not measured for this system; confirm binding has reached equilibrium given the on/off kinetics.
  • Path-length / inner-filter effects at 100 uL in a 96-well plate are not corrected; verify signal is linear with gSTEP concentration.
